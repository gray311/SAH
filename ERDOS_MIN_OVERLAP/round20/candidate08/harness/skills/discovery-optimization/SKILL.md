# Hyperparameter Search Strategy\n\n## Understanding the Problem\n\nThe seed optimizer uses 59000 training steps with 3 restarts. With only 30 eval budget, we cannot train many candidates.\n\n## Strategy: Systematic Hyperparameter Search\n\n### Key Hypotheses to Test:\n\n1. **Fewer restarts, longer training**: num_restarts=1 allows full 59k steps per candidate. With 3 restarts, effective budget is ~20k steps.\n\n2. **Higher penalty_strength**: Forces h(x) to [0,1] range. Seed uses 61.0. Try 100-500.\n\n3. **Learning rate tuning**: base_learning_rate=0.0062 may be suboptimal. Try 0.01-0.03.\n\n4. **Resolution (num_intervals)**: 800 may be too coarse. Try 1600 for higher precision.\n\n## Workflow\n\n1. CALL edit_solution to change ONE hyperparameter\n2. Set num_restarts=1, seed_start=0 (single evaluation)\n3. CALL evaluate_solution and record combined_score\n4. Compare to current best, keep improvements\n5. Repeat with different parameters\n\n## Editing Rules\n\n- Change only ONE field per edit\n- Keep other critical fields from seed (num_steps=59000, domain=[0,2])\n- Ensure integral constraint handling is preserved\n\n## Expected Outcomes\n\nBy exhaustively searching hyperparameter space, we should find configurations that beat the seed score. The seed may be stuck in local optimum from default hyperparameters.
