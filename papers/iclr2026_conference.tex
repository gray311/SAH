\begin{abstract}
Agent self-improvement---getting better at hard problems from one's own
experience, with no stronger model to learn from---takes two forms today.  Test-time RL fine-tunes the executor on its own rollouts: its gains are
diffuse---attributable to no nameable tool, skill, or procedure---and
unavailable once the executor is frozen by policy or served behind an API.  Harness editing instead revises the prompts, tools,
and control loop around a frozen model, but with a \emph{fixed} proposer that
re-derives its design knowledge from scratch on every run.  We propose to
learn the proposer itself.  \method{} keeps the executor permanently frozen
and trains the policy that \emph{proposes harnesses}: per task it synthesizes a
complete harness---prompt, skills, control parameters, new executable
tools---compiles it into a runnable agent, and learns from the frozen
executor's rollout scores alone, through an RLOO objective with
gap-normalized, best-of-$K$-sharpened rewards.  Generated code passes a leakage-controlled review chain, so improvements
stay attributable to inspectable artifacts.  Across eleven problems---mathematical construction, heuristic-contest
programming, systems optimization---the learned proposer carries a frozen 9B executor past
the discovery-finetuned Finch-9B on all eleven and sets the published
$\le$10B state of the art on five---including the Erd\H{o}s minimum-overlap
problem, where the frozen executor, steered only by a learned harness,
surpasses the best known human construction.  A breakthrough ledger traces each major jump to an identifiable
mechanism---a designed protocol, sampling breadth, compounding inheritance,
or new information.
\end{abstract}

\subsubsection*{Ethics statement}
This work automates the construction of agent harnesses, including
machine-written tool code that executes inside an evaluation loop.  We treat
the attendant risks---reward hacking and evaluation-integrity failures---as
first-class design constraints rather than afterthoughts: generated code cannot read the
evaluator implementation or ground-truth data and reaches scores only through a
budgeted interface, is statically gated,
sandbox-tested, and repaired only under capability parity with a localization
guard (\S\ref{sec:method-safety}).  The tasks are mathematical and
systems-optimization benchmarks with no human subjects or personal data.

\subsubsection*{Reproducibility statement}
The executor is frozen throughout, campaign artifacts (per-round candidate
specs from round~r312 onward, review logs, rollout scores, and the provenance
ledger underlying Table~\ref{tab:main_results}) are retained, and the spec language,
gates, reviewer, reward computation, and round orchestration are released as
code.  Appendix~\ref{app:h2spec}--\ref{app:infra} list the spec schema, the
capability surface, hyperparameters, and the aarch64 evaluation protocol for
the AHC tasks.
