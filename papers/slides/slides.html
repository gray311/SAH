Trick — GRPO reward (gap-normalized + rescue)

r = (score − base) / (ceiling − base)

fraction of the remaining gap closed; RLOO baseline.

v3 rescue: a K-tied batch that all beat the frontier still gets signal — not zeroed as "no signal."

Rescues AC1, where 71% of rounds gave zero gradient.

Trick · reward
10

How the GRPO reward is computed

r   = (score − base) / (ceiling − base)      gap closed · soft-cap 3·tanh
A_k = r_k − mean(r₋ₖ)                    RLOO baseline · no std blow-up
    + α·(softmax(r/τ) − 1/K)             max-weighted · banks best-of-K
tied group → A = 0, skip round          zero-signal filter

Discovery banks the best of K, not the mean.

20

Lesson — rollouts stay parallel

A GRPO group needs K i.i.d. samples from one policy — so A = (r − mean)/std is valid.

Sequential (sample k sees 0..k−1) makes them dependent → breaks the group advantage.

Also 7× slower (serial). Rejected.

Diversity belongs in temperature / forced-tool / dedup — not in the rollouts.
