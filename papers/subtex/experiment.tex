\section{Experiments}
\label{sec:experiments}

\subsection{Experimental Setup}
\label{sec:exp-setup}

\paragraph{Evaluation Tasks.}  Eleven discovery problems in three families,
drawn from the evaluation suite of the discovery-finetuned Finch
models~\citep{finch} so that a matched-scale finetuned baseline exists for
every task: \emph{mathematical construction} (\textbf{Erd\H{o}s},
\textbf{AC1}, \textbf{AC2}, \textbf{CP}, \textbf{Hadamard}), popularized by
AlphaEvolve and its
replications~\citep{alphaevolve,alphaevolvemath,openevolve};
\emph{heuristic-contest programming} (\textbf{ahc039}, \textbf{ahc058};
\emph{Algorithm Engineering} in Table~\ref{tab:main_results}), scored under
the official AtCoder 150-case protocol~\citep{ahc,alebench}; and
\emph{systems algorithm design} (\textbf{EPLB}, \textbf{PRISM},
\textbf{LLM-SQL}, \textbf{Txn}; \emph{System Performance}) from the ADRS
suite~\citep{barbarians}.  Every task ships a task description, a seed
program with an editable region, a black-box evaluator, and a hard
per-rollout budget of evaluator calls; scores are reported in each task's
native units, and the executor never sees the evaluator's implementation or
ground truth (\S\ref{sec:method-safety}).

\paragraph{Baselines.}  \emph{Finch-9B}~\citep{finch}, a 9B model
discovery-finetuned on evolution-trajectory corpora, is the matched-scale
external bar: beating it is the evidence that a learned harness substitutes
for discovery finetuning.  \emph{Published $\le$10B SOTA} aggregates the
best previously reported score per task across systems at or below 10B
parameters (sources in the notes of Table~\ref{tab:main_results}); because
it pools many specialized systems against one frozen executor, we read it as
calibration.  Our own system appears under three conditions that share the
executor, evaluator, seed program, initial harness, and per-rollout budget,
and differ only in what the reward updates: \emph{\method~(initial)}, a
fixed hand-written harness---no adaptation; \emph{\method~(context)},
proposer weights frozen, adaptation only through what the proposer
reads---the incumbent, the experience digest, and a leak-guarded analyst
brief distilled by the same frozen executor; and \emph{\method~(weight)},
the trained proposer---our full method.

\paragraph{Implementation Details.}  The executor is
Qwen3.5-9B~\citep{qwen3}, served frozen with vLLM~\citep{vllm}; no gradient
or test-time update ever touches it.  For the outer search, we sample
$K{=}8$ harness specs per task per round at 20--25 evaluator calls per
rollout; cascade rounds screen $K{=}16$ candidates at 5 calls each and
promote the top four to full budget.  For proposer training, we attach a
LoRA adapter~\citep{lora} (rank 64, $\alpha{=}128$; 116.4M trainable
parameters) to the same base model and run offline-replay RLOO
(Eq.~\eqref{eq:advantage}) for 3 epochs per step at global batch size 8,
with the adapter persisting across steps and a KL anchor to the frozen
base.  Winning programs are re-evaluated five times for stability.  Full
hyperparameters, per-round costs, and the aarch64 evaluation protocol are
presented in Appendix~\ref{app:infra}.
