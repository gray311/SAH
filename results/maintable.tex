x86 testers fail silently under qemu-user emulation).
$^\ddagger$Erd\H{o}s: latest long-trajectory campaign (round~1403,
$M_{\phi}$ retrained to \texttt{erdosmin\_03}) reached raw min-overlap
$0.380934$ (combined $0.999968$), within $2\times10^{-6}$ of the
previous $\leq$10B SOTA ($0.380932$).
